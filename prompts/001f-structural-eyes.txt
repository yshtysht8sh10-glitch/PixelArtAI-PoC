Create a 16x16 pixel-art sprite.

Object:
A cute red slime viewed from the front.

Available palette:
0 = transparent
1 = black
2 = red
3 = white

Required body structure:
- The slime must be one connected, centered body.
- The body must have a rounded/blob-like silhouette: narrower near the top and wider near the bottom.
- The interior of the body must be primarily red.
- Leave transparent space around the sprite.
- Do not place isolated pixels away from the body.

Required eye structure:
- The slime must have exactly two eyes: one left eye and one right eye.
- The two eyes must be separate groups of black pixels.
- The left eye and right eye must never touch each other.
- There must be at least one red pixel between the left eye and the right eye on every row where both eyes appear.
- Do not connect the two eyes with black pixels.
- Each eye should be small relative to the body.
- Place the eyes symmetrically around the vertical center line of the sprite.
- White may be used only as a small highlight inside an eye.
- Do not create one large black rectangle in the center of the face.

Output rules:
- Output exactly 16 lines.
- Each line must contain exactly 16 characters.
- Use only 0, 1, 2, or 3.
- Each character represents exactly one pixel.
- Do not output Markdown code fences.
- Do not output any explanation or extra text.
